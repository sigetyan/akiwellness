About Aki

Aki Irikawa is a physiotherapist licensed in Japan (理学療法士) and has been treating people for more than 16 years. She lives in Niseko and knows the aches a ski week leaves behind: tired quads, a stiff lower back, the shoulder you landed on.

Every treatment starts with a quick chat about where it hurts and how hard you want the pressure. She's also certified in CPR by the Japanese Red Cross.

Questions

Do I need to speak Japanese?

No. Aki speaks English, and booking, payment and the treatment itself all happen in English.

Which areas of Niseko do you cover?

Hirafu (upper and lower), Hanazono, Annupuri, Niseko Village, Moiwa and Kutchan town. If you're staying further out, send a message and Aki will let you know.

What do I need to prepare?

A clear patch of floor about 2 m by 1 m. Aki brings the massage table, towels and oil. Shower beforehand if you've just come off the mountain.

How does payment work?

You pay a ¥10,000 deposit online by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay when you book. Prices are in yen and your card converts them. The balance is paid on the day of your treatment.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancelling on the day of the treatment is charged at 100%. Cancelling the day before is charged at 50%. Cancel two or more days ahead and your deposit is refunded.

Can Aki help with a ski injury?

Aki is a Japan licensed physiotherapist, so she can assess and work on sore muscles, tight hips, knees and backs after skiing or hiking. For suspected fractures, head knocks or anything serious, see a clinic first.

How far ahead should I book?

Same day bookings are often possible, especially early afternoon. In peak season, from Christmas through February, book a few days ahead for evening slots.
